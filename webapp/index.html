Webcam Eye Tracker

Look at any number 1–40 with your eyes. The dot will follow.

You'll see brief instructions next, then a 14-second calibration.

How it works

  1. Grant camera access. When you click Start tracking below, your browser will ask for permission. Your video never leaves your device.
  2. Sit comfortably about an arm's length away from your screen, with even lighting on your face. Avoid backlight from a window.
  3. Calibrate (~14 seconds). A yellow-pulsing number will appear. Look directly at it and hold your gaze. After 1.5 seconds the next one lights up. The walkthrough visits 9 numbers spread across the grid.
  4. Track. A green dot follows your gaze and the number cell you're looking at lights up green.
  5. Refine on the fly. If the dot is off (e.g. it shows 21 but you're really looking at 5), click the correct number. The model learns from each click and gets better over time.
  6. Keyboard: press Esc any time to stop.

Tip: cell 8 shows your live webcam preview so you can check your framing.
