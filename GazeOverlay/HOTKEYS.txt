================================================================
  GazeOverlay  -  Hotkey Reference
================================================================

GLOBAL HOTKEYS (work even while a game / browser is focused)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  F2   F2 BURST: 10 seconds of high-weight click-to-correct.
       Every click during this window is treated as a strong
       "user was looking here" signal. Use right after calibration
       to refine quickly, or any time accuracy drops noticeably.

  F3   Toggle CONTINUOUS CALIBRATION on/off.
       Default is ON after calibration completes. Every real
       mouse click is treated as a potential calibration sample.
       Look for the floating +1 at each accepted click; faint x
       means rejected (gaze was elsewhere or unstable). Press F3
       to pause learning if you want the tracker to freeze.

  F8   Toggle overlay visibility (hide / show the gaze dot).
  F9   Start / stop session recording  ->  saves to sessions\
  F10  Quit the app.
  F11  Recalibrate from scratch (re-opens calibration window).


CALIBRATION WINDOW
----------------------------------------------------------------
  C    Lock eye spheres (look at the screen center first).

  After clicking 'Lock eye spheres', a fullscreen 9-point sequence
  runs (~15 seconds). Just follow the green dot with your eyes
  and keep your head still. These 9 points become "anchors" that
  protect the model from being warped by bad clicks later.

  If you've calibrated before, the 'Use saved calibration' button
  skips the 9-point sequence and reuses the saved model.


HOW CONTINUOUS LEARNING WORKS
----------------------------------------------------------------
While the overlay is running, every real mouse click anywhere on
the primary monitor is a potential calibration sample. The system
uses it only if ALL of these are true:
  - your face is visible to the webcam
  - your gaze has been stable (a fixation) for ~250 ms
  - your gaze is within ~500 px of where you clicked
  - the click is on the primary monitor

When accepted -> green '+1' floats up at the click position.
When rejected -> faint 'x' (gaze was elsewhere, or unstable).

Samples are binned by screen region (4x3 grid, max 8 per bin LRU)
so one corner can't dominate the fit. Anchors from the 9-point
calibration are never evicted. Outliers are pruned periodically.

The saved model auto-updates as you use the app:
  C:\Users\<you>\.gaze_overlay_calibration.json
Delete that file (or press F11) to start fresh.


TYPICAL SESSION FLOW
----------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Launch GazeOverlay.exe
  2. Look at the screen center, click 'Lock eye spheres' (or C)
  3. Follow the green dot for 9 points (~15 sec)
  4. Use your computer normally -- browse, play, anything.
     Each accepted click shows a +1, and accuracy gradually
     improves over the first few minutes.
  5. (Optional) F9 to start a session recording
  6. (Optional) F2 if you want a fast refinement burst
  7. F10 to quit -- the refined model auto-saves


WHERE FILES ARE STORED
----------------------------------------------------------------
  Next to GazeOverlay.exe:
    sessions\session_YYYYMMDD_HHMMSS.csv   <- gaze data per session
    app.log                                <- diagnostic log

  In your user folder:
    .gaze_overlay_calibration.json         <- model + samples
                                              (auto-updates as you click)

  CSV columns:
    timestamp, screen_x, screen_y, yaw_deg, pitch_deg,
    ear_left, ear_right, event

  event values:
    (empty)        normal sample
    fixation_start fixation began
    fixation_end   fixation ended
    saccade        rapid eye movement
    blink_start    blink began
    blink_end      blink ended


VIEWING A SESSION REPORT
----------------------------------------------------------------
  Open the CSV in Excel for raw data, OR run from a Python install:
    python report.py sessions\session_YYYYMMDD_HHMMSS.csv


TIPS
----------------------------------------------------------------
  - Continuous learning works best during normal browsing/gaming
    where you naturally look at things you click on. It rejects
    clicks where your eyes were already elsewhere (closing modals,
    muscle-memory clicks, etc.).
  - If accuracy is great in one screen area and bad in another,
    just look + click in the bad area a few times. The per-bin
    LRU keeps regional samples balanced.
  - If the tracker starts behaving worse over time (rare; bad
    lighting, partial face), press F11 for a fresh calibration --
    that wipes the click history.
  - Fullscreen-EXCLUSIVE games hide overlays. Switch the game to
    Borderless Windowed (or Windowed) in its video settings.
  - Webcam tracking accuracy is ~1-2 degrees. Good for awareness
    and slow inputs, NOT for FPS aim.
  - Status badge (top-left) tells you what is happening:
      "Tracking  (learning +N)"      = working, learned N samples
      "Tracking"                     = working
      "Continuous calibration: ON"   = F3 just toggled on
      "Continuous calibration: PAUSED (F3)" = learning is off
      "F2 burst ON (10s) ..."        = F2 burst mode active
      "Calibrate first  (see window)" = run calibration
      "No face detected"             = move into webcam view
      "Recording -> ..."             = saving samples to CSV
  - If anything goes wrong, check app.log next to the .exe.
