================================================================
  GazeOverlay  -  Hotkey Reference
================================================================

GLOBAL HOTKEYS (work even while a game / browser is focused)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  F2   F2 BURST: 10 seconds of strong click-to-correct
       Every click during this window adds a high-weight sample.
       Use right after calibration if accuracy needs fast tuning.

  F3   Toggle CONTINUOUS CALIBRATION on/off
       Default is ON. Every real click is treated as 'gaze was
       probably here' and refines the model. Look for the floating
       +1 marker at each accepted click. Press F3 to pause if you
       want the tracker to stop adapting for a while.

  F8   Toggle overlay visibility (hide / show the gaze dot)
  F9   Start / stop session recording  ->  saves to sessions\
  F10  Quit the app
  F11  Recalibrate from scratch (re-opens calibration window)


CALIBRATION WINDOW
----------------------------------------------------------------
  C    Lock eye spheres (look at the screen center first)

  After 'Lock eye spheres', a fullscreen 9-point sequence runs
  (~15 sec). Follow the green dot with your eyes; keep your head
  still. These 9 points become 'anchors' that never get evicted
  by continuous learning.

  If you've calibrated before, the 'Use saved calibration' button
  skips the 9-point sequence and reuses the saved model.


HOW CONTINUOUS LEARNING WORKS
----------------------------------------------------------------
While the overlay is running, every real mouse click anywhere on
the screen is a potential calibration sample. The system uses it
only if ALL of the following are true:
  - your face is visible to the webcam
  - your gaze has been stable (a fixation) for the last 250 ms
  - your gaze is within ~350 px of where you clicked
  - the click is on the primary monitor

When accepted -> '+1' floats up at the click position.
When rejected -> faint 'x' (gaze was elsewhere, or unstable).

Samples are binned by screen region (4x3 grid) with an LRU per
bin (max 8) so one corner can't dominate. Every ~5 refits the
system also drops outlier samples. Anchors from the 9-point
calibration are protected from eviction.

Press F3 to pause learning. The saved model in
  C:\Users\<you>\.gaze_overlay_calibration.json
keeps growing as you use the app.


TYPICAL SESSION FLOW
----------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Launch GazeOverlay.exe
  2. Look at screen center, click 'Lock eye spheres' (or C)
  3. Follow the green dot for 9 points (~15 sec)
  4. Use your computer normally -- browse, play, anything.
     Each accepted click shows a +1, and accuracy improves
     gradually over the first few minutes.
  5. (Optional) F9 to start a session recording
  6. F10 to quit -- the refined model auto-saves


WHERE FILES ARE STORED
----------------------------------------------------------------
  Next to GazeOverlay.exe:
    sessions\session_YYYYMMDD_HHMMSS.csv   <- gaze data per session
    app.log                                <- diagnostic log

  In your user folder:
    .gaze_overlay_calibration.json         <- model + samples
                                              (updates as you use it)

  CSV columns:
    timestamp, screen_x, screen_y, yaw_deg, pitch_deg,
    ear_left, ear_right, event


TIPS
----------------------------------------------------------------
  - Continuous calibration works best when you naturally look at
    things you click on (most everyday browsing/gaming behavior).
    It rejects clicks where your eyes were already elsewhere.
  - If accuracy is great in one area and bad in another, simply
    look + click in the bad area a few times. The LRU per-bin
    ensures regional samples count more than global ones.
  - If the tracker starts behaving worse over time (rare, can
    happen with bad lighting / partial face), press F11 for a
    fresh calibration and the click history is wiped.
  - Fullscreen-EXCLUSIVE games hide overlays. Switch the game to
    Borderless Windowed (or Windowed) in its video settings.
